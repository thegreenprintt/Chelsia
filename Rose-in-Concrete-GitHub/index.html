Why Rose in Concrete

A rose can grow where it was never expected to survive. Our youth can too.

A community-centered mentorship initiative helping youth and justice-impacted individuals recognize their potential, build resilience, and create a different path forward. Circumstances influence decisions. Opportunities shape outcomes. And the right person in your corner can change the trajectory of a life.

Out in the community
Our Mission

To provide mentorship and support to those who are justice-impacted, and develop preventive measures for vulnerable youth, fostering resilience and growth in the face of adversity.

Our Vision

A community where individuals impacted by the justice system can thrive, and vulnerable youth are guided and supported to prevent future incarceration by normalizing mental health support and building confidence.

On the field with the village
The Mentorship Program

A different kind of mentorship.

A 6-month, relationship-centered program for vulnerable and justice-impacted youth, built around credible messengers who connect through authenticity, lived experience, and trust. It isn't about talking at youth. It's about relationships strong enough for them to be heard, challenged, supported, and encouraged.

Credible Messengers

“I've been there. I understand. And I'm willing to walk with you.”

This isn't about authority. It's about connection. Our mentors bring lived experience, understanding, accountability, and hope — and they complete training before they ever serve.

Become a Credible Messenger
The Standard

Show up. Listen. Connect. Challenge. Encourage. Follow through.

A credible messenger uses authentic connection, relatability, and lived experience to guide young people. Sometimes a young person needs to see someone who looks like them, understands them, and made it through. That connection opens doors that authority never could.

Relate to their experiences
Speak honestly about their own journey
Build trust over time
Challenge them without judging them
Help them see possibilities
Walk alongside them, not talk at them
